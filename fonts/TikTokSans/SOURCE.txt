The static files are instances of the variable font
TikTokSans[opsz,slnt,wdth,wght].ttf at optical size 36, width 100, slant 0:

- TikTokSans-Regular.ttf    weight 400
- TikTokSans-Medium.ttf     weight 500
- TikTokSans-SemiBold.ttf   weight 600
- TikTokSans-Bold.ttf       weight 700

ffmpeg drawtext loads TikTokSans-SemiBold.ttf directly. The text slideshow
uses the same files through Pillow. Weight 600 matched the Brochi tip titles
and weight 500 matched the body copy.
